A 403 error is often caused by a cloudflare challenge.
Due to this project not being even close to warranting a proper solution to this,
  there is instead a workaround.

1) Go to any meta.miraheze.org page and open your browser's development tools, then
   go to the network tab and reload the page.
2) Click on the first request, find the request headers, and copy the value of the
   "cookie" header.
3) Create a file named ".cookie" and paste the cookie header contents in this file.

You do not need to restart the program.

(If you aren't happy with this, you are more than welcome to submit a PR with a real
  [read: NOT READING COOKIES AUTOMATICALLY] solution)
